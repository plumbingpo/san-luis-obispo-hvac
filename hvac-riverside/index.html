Keeping Your Riverside HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
